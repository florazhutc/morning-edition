Actionable Insight

Actionable Insight

This discussion reveals that Rust's pursuit of optimal performance is complicated by its internal architecture and design philosophy. The loss of high-level semantic invariants during compilation (HIR to MIR) critically hinders the compiler's ability to perform advanced, proof-based optimizations like skipping bounds checks.
