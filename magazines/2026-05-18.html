💰 AI Cost Analysis

Apple Silicon's Hidden Costs Make Local AI Pricier Than OpenRouter

Offline Agentic Coding part 3: Apple Silicon costs more than OpenRouter. Apple silicon costs more than OpenRouter. At ~50-100 watts under load, and ~$0.20 per kWh, my M5 MacbookPro will cost a few cents per hour. Accelerated depreciation (if any) from shortening the lifespan of the device will be more expensive than the electricity. At a few tens of tokens per second this works out to ammortized costs of ~$1.50 per million tokens.

Actionable Insight

This article presents a counter-intuitive analysis, arguing that the total cost of running AI models locally on Apple Silicon, especially when factoring in accelerated depreciation, can surpass the expense of using cloud APIs like OpenRouter. This challenges the common assumption that local inference is always cheaper, underscoring the necessity of a comprehensive cost assessment beyond mere electricity consumption for AI deployment strategies. It suggests that the perceived savings of local hardware might be offset by its wear and tear, potentially making cloud solutions more economically viable for many intensive AI tasks.

Community Voice

The Hacker News community largely dismisses the original post's analysis as flawed, citing issues like unfairly attributing the entire laptop cost to token generation, inflated electricity estimates, and the critical omission of "free" input tokens for local inference. While some concede that hosted solutions *might* appear cheaper purely for output tokens, the community emphasizes the overlooked value of local inference's privacy, censorship-free nature, and the laptop's multi-purpose utility.
