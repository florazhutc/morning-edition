Software Development 💻

Despite AI's Coding Advancements, Software Quality Continues to Decline

Source: original article

Nothing Works and Everyone Is Euphoric | ptrchm As I’m writing this, we’re in the middle of an AI-induced mass psychosis. People are literally token-maxxing themselves into hospital beds , scrambling to capture some of that market value before everything is automated away. Models keep getting better, programmers are being laid off left and right. We’ve been repeatedly told that AI will write 100% of the code by the end of the year.

Actionable Insight

The tech industry is experiencing a paradox where AI is rapidly improving coding capabilities, leading to predictions of full automation and programmer layoffs, yet the overall quality and user experience of software are perceived to be deteriorating. This suggests a significant disconnect between the technical ability to generate code and the actual delivery of reliable, user-friendly software. The challenges in software development appear to extend beyond mere code production, encompassing design, user focus, and organizational priorities.

Community Voice

Commenters widely lament the decline in software quality, expressing dread over updates that often worsen user experience, citing examples like macOS and Slack. Many attribute this to misaligned organizational incentives, where non-technical decision-makers prioritize 'shiny new tools' and feature-driven promotions over stability and user needs. A recurring theme is that 'code quality' does not equate to 'software quality,' with the core problems identified as conflicting requirements, resource constraints, haste, and a lack of accountability for software failures, rather than the act of coding itself, even with AI assistance.
