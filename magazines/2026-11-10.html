Notice about the Fireworks AI incident that may impact some Notion workspaces – Notion Help Center This notice is about the security incident at one of our vendors, Fireworks AI, that may impact some Notion workspaces. Following an investigation by the Notion security team, we have determined that <1% of Notion workspaces may have been affected by the Fireworks security incident. Actionable Insight

A security incident at Fireworks AI, a Notion vendor, resulted in the exposure of unattributable Notion AI chat transcripts from a small fraction (<1%) of Notion workspaces. This incident highlights the inherent supply chain risks when integrating third-party AI services, even when data is de-identified. It also raises questions about the transparency and enforcement of data retention policies with external vendors.

Community Voice

Hacker News commenters questioned the discrepancy between Fireworks AI's advertised 'private' data retention policy and the actual exposure of stored chat data. Many expressed skepticism about the 'unattributable' nature of the leaked transcripts, finding it concerning that chat content itself was exposed regardless of direct user linkage. The official incident response was largely criticized as insufficient, and some users debated the overall value and usage of Notion's AI features.
