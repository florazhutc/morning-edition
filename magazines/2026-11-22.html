Inkling 2 Mini: New Looped Transformer Model Excels on Benchmarks and Runs Locally

Source: original article

Introducing Inkling 2 Mini - Thinking Machines Lab Today we are releasing Inkling 2 Mini, a fast, powerful model that’s small enough to run on your laptop. Inkling 2 Mini is a looped transformer, running the middle layers of the model three times for every token. This allows the model to apply more reasoning and reach the right answer with fewer tokens, especially on tasks such as agentic coding that depend on reasoning quality. Inkling 2 Mini leads every open-weights model with less than 100B total parameters on benchmarks such as DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%) and Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%).

Actionable Insight

Inkling 2 Mini introduces a novel looped transformer architecture, which re-runs middle layers multiple times per token to enhance reasoning. This design enables the model to achieve high accuracy with fewer tokens, particularly benefiting complex tasks like agentic coding. Its performance on benchmarks positions it as a leading open-weights model under 100 billion parameters.

Community Voice

The community expresses significant excitement, with many users reporting promising real-world performance, especially for local deployment on consumer hardware like MacBooks and Ryzen systems, achieving decent token generation speeds. Users highlight the model's novel open-weights looped transformer architecture as a potential game-changer, suggesting it could influence future open-source models and even hardware integration. Early tests, including coding tasks and data analytics exams, show strong results comparable to larger models, leading some to compare its impact to the Stable Diffusion breakthrough. The availability of GGUF versions for tools like Ollama/Llama.cpp is also noted, with some speculating its potential for local multi-agent reasoning harnesses.
