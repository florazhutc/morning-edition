Actionable Insight

Italy's parliament has voted to re-enable nuclear energy development, specifically focusing on Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) and other advanced technologies. This legislation establishes the necessary regulatory framework for future projects rather than authorizing immediate construction. The move marks a significant policy reversal for Italy, which previously banned nuclear power, and could influence broader European energy security discussions.

Commenters also noted a broader international trend of countries re-evaluating or expanding nuclear energy, alongside concerns about the politicization of nuclear power and the potential cost burden of SMRs on taxpayers.

💻 Hardware & OS

Qualcomm Announces Linux Support for Snapdragon X2 Series, Upstreaming Core Drivers

Source: original article

Inside Snapdragon Summit 2026: Agentic AI PCs, Googlebooks and Linux

Actionable Insight

Qualcomm's commitment to upstreaming core drivers for the Snapdragon X2 Series, including NPU and GPU, marks a significant step towards enabling robust Linux support on its high-performance ARM platform. This move addresses a long-standing desire from the Linux community for powerful ARM-based laptops, potentially offering a compelling alternative to Apple's M-series and traditional x86 architectures. However, the success hinges on consistent upstream contributions and broad OEM adoption to ensure a reliable user experience.

Community Voice

The community expresses cautious optimism, emphasizing the critical need for Qualcomm to upstream all device tree and kernel-level drivers to ensure long-term support and prevent fragmentation, a common issue with ARM hardware. Many acknowledge the Snapdragon X2 series' impressive performance, viewing it as the closest competitor to Apple's M-series, and express a strong desire for high-performance Linux laptops. However, there's skepticism due to past unfulfilled promises regarding Linux support on ARM chips, highlighting a need for Qualcomm to rebuild trust. Initial OpenBSD support for X2 Elite laptops is noted, and users appreciate the clarity that support is focused on X2 series laptops, though some lament the exclusion of desktops or older platforms.
