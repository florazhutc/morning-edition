WhatsApp's Help Pages Contained Hidden LLM Instructions to Avoid Recommending Regulators

Source: original article

WhatsApp's Hidden Notes Told AI Not to Recommend a Regulator Until mid-October 2026, WhatsApp's help pages told an AI not to recommend the regulator, and not to cite that law once you'd mentioned a lawyer. You couldn't see the second part. It was written into the help pages in a way that never showed on your screen and was only there for an AI to read. We found it on October 8, 2026.

Actionable Insight

WhatsApp's help pages included hidden instructions for an AI, directing it not to recommend regulators or cite specific laws. This incident highlights a lack of transparency in how companies configure AI chatbots, particularly concerning sensitive legal and regulatory advice. The discovery raises concerns about user trust and the ethical deployment of AI in customer support roles.

Community Voice

The community is divided, with many commenters agreeing that instructions preventing a chatbot from offering legal advice or recommending regulators are sensible for a company to avoid liability. However, others express disappointment and skepticism regarding Meta's transparency and ethics, viewing the incident as 'shenanigans' or a concerning display of corporate behavior, despite WhatsApp's claim that the instructions were internal and displayed due to a bug.

Mistral Unveils Timestral, Efficient Time-Series Foundation Models

Source: original article

Timestral: Mistral's time-series foundation models | Mistral Timestral, Mistral’s first family of time-series foundation models, delivers state-of-the-art probabilistic, multivariate forecasts with just 209M parameters, outperforming larger models on key benchmarks while supporting past and future covariates. Available in four sizes (5M to 209M parameters), it balances accuracy and efficiency, running efficiently on CPUs and excelling in zero-shot forecasting for diverse domains. The model combines synthetic pre-training, covariate-aware attention, and long-context learning, with fine-tuning options for domain adaptation via Mistral Forge. Today, we’re releasing Timestral, our first family of time-series foundation models.

Actionable Insight

Mistral's Timestral family introduces efficient, state-of-the-art time-series foundation models, delivering strong probabilistic, multivariate forecasts with significantly fewer parameters than competitors. Its design emphasizes balancing accuracy and efficiency, enabling CPU deployment and strong zero-shot performance across various domains. The models leverage synthetic pre-training and covariate-aware attention, with fine-tuning capabilities for domain adaptation.

Community Voice

The community expresses both excitement and skepticism regarding Timestral. While some users highlight the potential for significant efficiency gains in forecasting multiple series and appreciate the speed of deployment, others question the generalizability of a "foundation model" for diverse time-series data without domain-specific knowledge. Concerns also include the difficulty of out-of-sample testing for slow-generating data and a restrictive license, with some users drawing parallels to the generalizability of LLMs like ChatGPT while others express disappointment with prior Mistral models.

Actionable Insight

The rapid adoption of AI for code generation is creating a 'black box' problem for engineers, who feel increasingly uncomfortable shipping code they don't fully understand. The pace of development with AI tools makes thorough line-by-line review impractical, leading to systems where high-level understanding supersedes detailed knowledge.
