Actionable Insight

Cloudflare's acquisition of Deno appears to be an 'acquihire,' as the company announced it will cease Deno runtime development after one year of support. Source: original article

Someone Rebuilt Free, Open-Source Versions of Photoshop, Premiere, and Lightroom Using AI | PetaPixel Software engineer Brandon Thomas has built and released an open-source, free alternative to Adobe’s Creative Cloud suite, including versions of Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, Lightroom (not Classic), Acrobat, After Effects, and InDesign. The apps live under the ArtCraft banner and are available for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Thomas founded ArtCraft nearly a year ago and previously worked as CEO of Storyteller.ai and as a software engineer at Square. He writes on Reddit that he used Anthropic’s Opus 5.5 AI LLM to recreate seven of Adobe’s most popular apps in Rust.

Actionable Insight

This ambitious project leverages Rust for performance and Anthropic's Opus 5.5 AI LLM in its development, showcasing AI's potential in accelerating complex software engineering tasks. The initiative offers a significant open-source alternative for creative professionals across macOS, Windows, and Linux platforms.

Creator Recreates Original 2D Physics Engine Behind Grand Theft Auto

Source: original article

One weekend in late August 1996, I wrote a 2D physics simulation which later became the basis of the vehicle system in the eventually popular game "Grand Theft Auto" aka "GTA". It began as a little wireframe demo written in GFA BASIC on my Atari ST home computer (and was later ported to C for use in the actual game). I have recreated* it here in JavaScript for its 30th anniversary in a slightly prettier, "remastered" form, for anyone who is interested in seeing or playing it. You can play the recreation above, with touch controls if you are on mobile. In addition to the default "Car" mode (1), you can also select the technically simpler Spaceship aka "Ship" (2) and "Brick" (3) modes, either via the mode selector in Settings or by pressing the corresponding numbers on your keyboard.

Actionable Insight

The author has recreated the 2D physics simulation originally developed in 1996 for the Atari ST, which later became the vehicle system for 'Grand Theft Auto.' This recreation, celebrating its 30th anniversary, underscores the foundational role of early, isolated prototypes in game development. It offers a playable demonstration of how simple 'toy' projects can evolve into core mechanics for highly successful games.

Community Voice

The community largely expresses nostalgia and appreciation for the original GTA I and II car physics, noting its unique 'fun' and 'great feel' compared to later 3D versions. Many commenters highlight the importance of prototyping 'toys' in product development, allowing for experimentation with isolated concepts. Some users shared their own experiences with 2D car physics engines or referenced other foundational models, while others expressed gratitude for the engine's impact on their gaming experiences. Actionable Insight

This article aims to provide a guide to C for developers who learned Rust as their first systems programming language. The author, having started with Rust, offers a unique perspective on C's characteristics and 'quirks.' This initiative addresses a gap in existing documentation, which typically caters to C/C++ programmers transitioning to Rust.
